# Sterlyn visual system
Forest #0E3025, ink #10271F, champagne #C9A068, ivory #F6F2E9, pearl #FFFDF7, charcoal #1F1F1F. Gold is an accent, never the body text on ivory.
Cormorant Garamond display, Manrope interface, self-hosted. Display type is editorial and restrained. Wide quiet margins, architectural hairlines, asymmetric image compositions. Dark hero transitions into warm ivory editorial rooms.
Hero: scroll-controlled framed portal, exact source-derived 3D monogram, ten-second rotation and illumination cycle. Motion clarifies hierarchy and state. No particles, neon, decorative dashboard gradients, fabricated statistics, template feature-card rows, or fake testimonials.
Operate surfaces: compact sidebar, clear tables, visible filters and pending states. Mobile uses its own stacked compositions and touch controls. All UI supports reduced motion and keyboard use.
